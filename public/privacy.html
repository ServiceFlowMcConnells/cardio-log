Training Log privacy policy

Training Log is a personal training, nutrition and body-composition log. This page explains what it does with your data.

Health Connect data

  • What it reads: daily step counts and sleep sessions (start, end and sleep stages), only after you allow it in Health Connect.
  • Why: to show your steps against your step target, and your sleep alongside your readiness check-ins and training.
  • Where it goes: a daily summary (steps, minutes asleep and in each stage) is saved to your own Training Log account so you can see it on your phone and laptop. Raw Health Connect records are not stored.
  • What it never does: write to Health Connect, share your data with anyone, use it for advertising, or sell it.
  • Stopping: remove Training Log's access in Health Connect at any time. Summaries already saved stay in your account until you delete them.

Your account

Your logs are stored in a private database (Supabase) that only your signed-in account can read. Food searches use Open Food Facts; barcode scans send only the barcode.
